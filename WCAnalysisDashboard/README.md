# **World Cup Analysis Dashboard - Personal Project (In Progress)**
**Date:** September 2026 - Present


## **Introduction**
In this project, I use Hudl Statsbomb data from the 2022 World Cup to create a dashboard that displays and visualizes performance in the tournament across three areas: Match Analysis, Team Analysis and Player Analysis. Using Shiny App via Python, the dashboard has three tabs that cover the listed areas of analysis and incorporates its features, tables and visualizations to display performance in those areas. I also use Jupyter Notebook to organize the data collection, data transformation, and development of visualizations used in the app. The goal of the dashboard is to provide an in depth and technical analysis of performance of players and teams in individual matches and in the tournament as a whole while utilizing visualizations to understand where teams excelled. 

UPDATE: The app now contains both Match Analysis and Player Analysis. Team Analysis is currently being developed and tested. 


## **Data Collection & Visualization Development**
As mentioned, I use Jupyter Notebook to assist in collecting the data from Statsbomb's free data API and transforming it into various datasets for use in the dashboard. This dashboard particularly uses event level data and schedule data from the Statsbomb's API and uses different methods to aggregate or organize the data into statistics at both the game and tournament level. Not every dataset used in the dashboard is from the notebook and may have involved manual data collection from external sources (examples: player mapping, rosters). Furthermore, the notebook also creates methods that develop initial ideas for how the visualizations will look on the actual dashboard. Note that the actual methods may not look exact in the final dashboard's code and will be changed to match the format needed to work with Shiny's features. 


## **Dashboard Features**
The dashboards attempts to display and visualize performance in the following areas:
- **Match Analysis:** This section analyzes each individual match in the tournament, navigable using a "Round" and "Match" selection. This section has four subsections. Team Stats looks at both team's statistics in four performance areas: Attacking, Ball progression, Defending and Discipline. Lineups visualizes both teams starting formations, starting lineup, and substitutions made during the game. Player Stats displays tables for both outfield players and goalkeepers with the outfield table having four sections to view: Standard, Attacking, Possession & Distribution and Defending. Lastly, Match Visuals contains four visualizations for the match: xT Momentum, Cumulative xG, Shot Map and Penalty Analysis for in game penalties and penalty shootouts.
- **Player Analysis:** This section analyzes each player's performance in individual matches and aggregated throughout the tournament. This section has three subsections. Player Overview contains tables that highlight the player's statistics throughout the tournament and in each match, aggregated by four sections for outfield players: Standard, Attacking, Possession & Distribution and Defending. Player Visualizations contains three different visualizations: Event Heat Map, Player Shot Map (Shots faced for Keepers), and Pass Map. Similar Performances uses a dataset created via KNN clustering to find similar statistical performances with other players in the same position group (Similarity is based on statistical performances during the tournament, not playing style or player archetype).
- **Team Analysis (IN PROGRESS):** This section is intended to highlight each team's performance in individual matches and throughout the tournament. This section will contain 4 subsections: Rosters, Matches, Leaderboards, and Team Visualizations. Rosters will display the team's full roster and highlight how many minutes each player played in the tournament. Matches will display statistic tables highlighting how the team performed across the tournament and in each match individually. Leaderboards will look at key statistics and which players led for their team in that statistic over the tournament. Lastly, Team Visualizations will contain visualizations highlighting performance in certain areas. The proposed visualizations are: Passing Network, Shot Map, Penalty Map, and Final Third Entrances.
